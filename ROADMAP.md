# Ciao Digital Signage Public Roadmap

This roadmap is intentionally high-level. Internal execution plans and private
operational runbooks are maintained outside public documentation.

## Near Term (0-3 months)

- Stability hardening for player runtime and CMS flows
- Better onboarding for webapp connectors
- Documentation improvements for deployment and architecture

## Mid Term (3-6 months)

- Expanded connector ecosystem
- Better observability and diagnostics
- UX improvements for scheduling and content workflows

## Long Term (6+ months)

- Advanced automation workflows
- Optional scalability enhancements for realtime and workloads
- Broader contributor ecosystem and integration templates

## How to Influence the Roadmap

Open a GitHub issue with:

1. Problem statement
2. Proposed outcome
3. Business/user impact
4. Constraints and alternatives considered
