# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to a welcoming and respectful community for everyone.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Assume good intent and discuss ideas, not people.
- Offer helpful feedback with clear technical reasoning.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, intimidation, or discrimination.
- Personal attacks, trolling, or inflammatory language.
- Publishing private information without consent.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions or interactions
that violate this code of conduct.

## Reporting

Report conduct issues privately to the maintainers.
